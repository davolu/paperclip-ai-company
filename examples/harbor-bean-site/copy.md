# Brand story
It all started in an old boat shed right by the harbor. Two friends shared a simple belief: great coffee brings neighbors together. With a lot of care and dedication, we started roasting small batches every Tuesday and Friday.

We aren't trying to reinvent the wheel. We just want to roast sweet, clean, honest coffee that feels like home. Stop by on a Tuesday or Friday, and see what happens when old friends turn a shared passion into a neighborhood ritual. We'll always have a cup waiting.
