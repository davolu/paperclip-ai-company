# Harbor Bean Co. — brand brief (fictional)

Harbor Bean Co. is a made-up neighborhood specialty roaster used only for this tutorial. There is no real shop, address, phone number or website.

- Tone: warm, friendly, short sentences. Coffee people talking to neighbors.
- Story seed: two friends roast small batches in an old boat shed by the harbor, every Tuesday and Friday.
- Three products:
  1. Harbor House — a smooth everyday filter blend (chocolate, toasted almond).
  2. Seasonal Pour-Over — a rotating single origin (this season: bright, citrus, honey).
  3. Low Tide Decaf — a Swiss-water decaf that still tastes like coffee.
- Hours: Mon–Fri 7:00–16:00, Sat 8:00–14:00, Sun closed.
- Location line: "By the old harbor, Example Town" (clearly fictional).
- Colours: deep navy, cream, a warm copper accent.
